    The Legend

    In 2009, a lonely taco was left under a heat lamp for 37 consecutive hours. Exposed to radioactive levels of ghost pepper and Mariachi music, it awoke with googly eyes, tiny arms, and a deep calling: to feed the people.

    Pete has since won zero (0) culinary awards, mostly because he keeps eating the trophies.

    FAQ

    Is Pete going to eat me?

    Nope! Pete is a strict tacotarian. He only eats tacos, which makes choosing lunch very easy.

    Is the Ghost Pepper Salsa actually that hot?

    It's our boldest salsa. Ask the team about its heat level before you try it.

    Do you have gluten-free options?

    Yes! All of our corn tortillas are gluten-free. Pete is a taco, not a menu item; please do not lick Pete.

    Can I bring my dog?

    Absolutely. Our patio is dog-friendly and Pete loves dogs. Dogs are confused by Pete.
