    FAQ

    Is Pete going to eat me?

    No. Pete is a strict tacotarian. He only eats tacos, which raises some questions we choose not to ask.

    Is the Ghost Pepper Salsa actually that hot?

    We make you sign a waiver. The waiver is printed on a napkin. You will need the napkin.

    Do you have gluten-free options?

    Yes! All of our corn tortillas are gluten-free. Pete is not. Please do not lick Pete.

    Can I bring my dog?

    Absolutely. Our patio is dog-friendly and Pete loves dogs. Dogs are confused by Pete.
